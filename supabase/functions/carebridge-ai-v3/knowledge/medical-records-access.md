# Medical records and test results

## What you can see in the app

- **My Appointments**: your past and upcoming visits and their status.
- **My Prescriptions**: the diagnosis, medicines, and doctor's notes from each completed visit.
- **Bills**: your bills and whether they are paid.
- **My Profile**: your contact details, allergies, and emergency contact.

The assistant can also look these up for you. It only sees your own records.

## Who else can see them

Your doctor sees the records of their own patients. Reception staff can see all records to run the clinic.

## Test results

The app does not store test results or lab reports. Collect your reports from the lab that did the test, and bring them to your next visit so your doctor can review them.

## Corrections and copies

- To report an error in a prescription or record, contact reception.
- To get a printed copy of your records or an invoice, ask reception.
