# Accounts and roles

## Creating an account

Sign up in the CareBridge app with an email and password, or use **Sign in with Google** on the login page. Every new account is a patient account.

## One person, one account

Each person needs their own account. Family members cannot share one account, and each patient books from their own account.

## Your profile

Open **My Profile** to update details such as your phone number, address, allergies, and emergency contact.

## Roles

There are three roles:

- **Patient**: book and manage appointments, join the waitlist, and see your prescriptions and bills.
- **Doctor**: see their schedule and their own patients' records, and complete consultations.
- **Receptionist**: confirm, reschedule, and cancel appointments, handle billing, and follow up on missed visits.

Doctor and receptionist roles are assigned by reception. You cannot change your own role.
