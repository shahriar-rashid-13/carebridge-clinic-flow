# Consultation fees

## How fees are set

Each doctor has their own consultation fee. Senior consultants charge more.

## Where to see a fee

- The doctor list in **Book Appointment** shows each doctor's fee, specialization, and working days.
- The booking summary shows the fee before you confirm.
- The assistant can look up a doctor's current fee for you.

Always check the current fee in the app. The fee shown there is the one that applies.

## Your bill

- Reception creates your bill after the visit.
- The bill total is built from the items on it, so it may include more than the consultation fee. Reception explains any extra item on request.
- You pay in cash at reception after the visit. Nothing is paid in advance.
