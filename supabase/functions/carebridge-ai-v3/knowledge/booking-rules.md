# Booking rules

## How to book

Open **Book Appointment** in the app. Choose a doctor, a date, and a time slot, and enter a short reason for the visit. The assistant can also prepare a booking card. Nothing is booked until you press **Confirm**.

## Rules

- You can only book one of the doctor's working days and one of their listed time slots.
- A slot that is already booked cannot be chosen. You can join the waitlist instead.
- Each patient books from their own account. Family members need their own accounts.
- You can book any future working day. There is no limit on how far ahead you book.

## Status

- **Requested**: reception has not confirmed the booking yet. Reception usually confirms it on the same or the next working day.
- **Confirmed**: shown in My Appointments. You get a reminder within the 24 hours before the visit.
- **Completed**: the consultation has taken place.

## Walk-ins

Walk-in patients are seen only if a slot is free. Booking in the app is preferred.

## On the day

- Bring photo ID, previous prescriptions, and recent test reports.
- If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, you may need to reschedule.
