# Reminders and email preferences

## Appointment reminders

- A reminder is sent within the 24 hours before a **confirmed** appointment. Requested bookings do not get reminders.
- Reminders appear under the bell icon in the app.
- If you have an email address on your profile, the reminder is also emailed to you.
- The clinic does not send SMS reminders.

Emails are not sent between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM (Dhaka time). An email due overnight arrives after 8:00 AM.

## Other notifications

The bell icon also shows waitlist offers and expired offers.

## Campaign emails

The clinic may email invitations, for example when a check-up or follow-up is due or after a missed visit. You get at most one campaign email every 7 days.

## Stopping emails

- Every campaign email has an **Unsubscribe** link. It stops campaign emails, and appointment reminders still arrive.
- On the same page you can tick **Also stop appointment reminder emails** to stop all emails.
- Reception can also record your choice. Contact reception to change it later.

In-app notifications under the bell icon continue either way.
