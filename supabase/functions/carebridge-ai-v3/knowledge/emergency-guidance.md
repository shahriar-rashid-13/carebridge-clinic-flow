# Emergency guidance

If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, signs of a stroke (face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech), or thoughts of harming yourself, call 999 immediately. This assistant cannot handle emergencies and cannot send help. Do not wait for a reply in the app.

## The clinic is not an emergency service

CareBridge Clinic runs booked outpatient appointments. For anything sudden or severe, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

## When it is urgent but not an emergency

- Contact reception on +880 1711-000000 during opening hours (Saturday to Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM).
- If you have side effects from a medicine, contact the clinic or your doctor. If they are severe, call 999.
- Do not use a waitlist or a future booking for a problem that needs care today.

## What the assistant can do

The assistant gives general information only. It does not diagnose, and it never tells you which medicine or dose to take.
