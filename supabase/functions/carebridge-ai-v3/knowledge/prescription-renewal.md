# Prescription renewal

## Getting a refill

To renew a prescription, book a follow-up appointment with your doctor. Prescriptions are only written during a consultation, so a doctor cannot renew one without a visit.

## Where to find your prescriptions

- Open **My Prescriptions** in the app.
- A prescription appears once the appointment is completed. The doctor writes it at the end of the consultation.
- Each appointment has one prescription.

A prescription shows the date, the doctor, the diagnosis, each medicine with its instructions, and the doctor's notes.

## Follow-up dates

Your doctor may set a follow-up date. If it passes without a visit, the clinic may email you a reminder to book, unless you have opted out of campaign emails.

## What the assistant cannot do

- The assistant cannot write, renew, or change a prescription. Only your doctor can.
- It will not tell you which medicine or dose to take.

## Problems

- To report an error on a prescription, contact reception.
- For side effects, contact the clinic or your doctor. In an emergency, call 999.
