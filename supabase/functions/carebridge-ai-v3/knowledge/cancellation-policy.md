# Cancellation policy

## How to cancel

Open **My Appointments** in the app and cancel the appointment. You can also ask the assistant. It prepares a cancellation card, and nothing changes until you press **Confirm** on that card.

## Which appointments can be cancelled

- Only appointments with status **requested** or **confirmed** can be cancelled.
- Completed appointments cannot be cancelled. Book a new follow-up appointment instead.
- Once cancelled, an appointment cannot be restored. Book again if you change your mind.

## Fees

There is no cancellation fee, and there is no minimum notice period. You can cancel at any time before the visit. Please cancel as early as you can so the slot can go to another patient.

## What happens to the freed slot

When you cancel, the slot is offered automatically to the next patient on the waitlist for that doctor and day.

## Cancelling for someone else

Each patient cancels from their own account. Reception staff can also cancel an appointment for you. Call +880 1711-000000 during opening hours.
