# Refund policy

## No advance payment

You do not pay when you book. Payment is made at reception after your visit, once your bill has been created. Because nothing is paid in advance, cancelling or rescheduling never costs you anything and there is nothing to refund.

## No refund process

The clinic does not have a refund process for completed consultations.

## If you think your bill is wrong

- Check your bill in the app. The assistant can show it to you.
- Contact reception on +880 1711-000000 during opening hours, or ask at the front desk on your next visit.
- Do not pay a bill you believe is wrong until reception has checked it.
- If you paid twice or were charged in error, reception checks the bill and returns the extra amount in cash.
