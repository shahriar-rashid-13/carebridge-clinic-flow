# Rescheduling policy

## How to reschedule

Open **My Appointments** and choose reschedule, or ask the assistant. The assistant prepares a card, and nothing changes until you press **Confirm**.

## Rules

- Only **requested** or **confirmed** appointments can be rescheduled.
- The new date cannot be in the past.
- You stay with the same doctor. To see a different doctor, cancel and book a new appointment.
- The new day must be one of the doctor's working days, and the new time must be one of the doctor's listed slots.
- The new slot must be free. A slot that someone else has booked cannot be chosen.

## After you reschedule

When you reschedule, the appointment goes back to **requested**. Reception then confirms it again. If reception moves the appointment for you, it stays **confirmed**.

Your old slot is then offered to the next patient on the waitlist.

## Completed visits

Completed appointments cannot be rescheduled. Book a follow-up appointment instead.

There is no fee for rescheduling, and there is no limit on how many times you reschedule.
