# Privacy

## Who can see your information

- **You** see only your own appointments, prescriptions, bills, and profile.
- **Doctors** see the records of their own patients.
- **Reception staff** can see all records to run the clinic.

These limits are enforced by the database for every screen and for the assistant. The assistant can only read what you are allowed to see.

## Booking privacy

When you look at free slots, the app shows which times are taken but never who booked them.

## The assistant

- Your chats with the assistant are private to your account.
- The assistant never makes a change on its own. You must press **Confirm** on its proposal card.
- Its general knowledge comes from anonymised records. It never names other patients.

## Emails

The clinic uses your email address for appointment reminders and, unless you opt out, for campaign emails. See the reminders and email preferences policy.

## Deleting your account

Contact reception on +880 1711-000000 to delete your account. Your medical and billing records may be kept after your account is closed, because the clinic needs them for its records. Ask reception for details.
