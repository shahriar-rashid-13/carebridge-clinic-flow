# Waitlist

## When to use it

Join the waitlist when the day or slot you want with a doctor is fully booked. If the slot you want is free, book it directly instead.

## Rules for joining

- Choose today or a future date on one of the doctor's working days.
- You can name one taken slot, or leave the slot open to accept any time that day.
- You can have one open waitlist entry per doctor and day.

## Offers

When a matching appointment is cancelled or moved, the slot is offered to the next waiting patient. Patients who asked for that exact slot come first, then the earliest to join.

- You get a notification under the bell icon, and the offer appears in **My Appointments**.
- You have **120 minutes** to accept the offer.
- An accepted offer becomes a booking with status **requested**, which reception then confirms.
- If you decline, or do not accept in time, the slot passes to the next patient. You stay on the waitlist for other slots that day.

## Leaving

You can leave the waitlist at any time from My Appointments. Entries for dates that have passed close automatically.
