# Opening hours and contact

## Hours

- **Saturday to Thursday:** 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM (Dhaka time).
- **Friday and public holidays:** closed.

Each doctor works on set days and times within these hours. The doctor list in **Book Appointment** shows each doctor's working days, and only their listed slots can be booked.

## Contact

- **Phone:** +880 1711-000000 (reception, during opening hours)
- **Address:** House 27, Road 9/A, Dhanmondi, Dhaka 1209

Reception staff speak Bangla and English.

## Getting here

- Only limited street parking is available.
- There is a wheelchair ramp and a lift at the entrance.

## Outside opening hours

You can still book, cancel, and reschedule in the app at any time. Reception confirms new bookings on the next working day. For emergencies, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Hours can change around public holidays and during Ramadan. Call reception to check before you travel.
