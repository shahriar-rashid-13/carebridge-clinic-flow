# No-show policy

## What counts as a no-show

A **confirmed** appointment that has not been completed 1 hour after its start time is flagged as a possible no-show.

## What happens next

- Reception reviews each possible no-show and records a note.
- Reception may contact you to rebook.
- You may receive an email inviting you to book again, unless you have opted out of campaign emails.

## Avoiding a no-show

- If you cannot come, cancel in **My Appointments** as early as you can. There is no cancellation fee, and the slot can go to a patient on the waitlist.
- If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, you may need to reschedule.
- Check that your booking is **confirmed**. Reminders are sent only for confirmed appointments.

## Rebooking

Book a new appointment in the app, or call reception on +880 1711-000000.

There is no fee for a missed appointment, and a missed visit does not stop you from booking again.
